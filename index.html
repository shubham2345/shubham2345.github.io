About

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at UC Santa Cruz, advised by Dr. Ian Lane. My research is on LLM post-training, factuality, and the safety-focused evaluation of language models and agents — where capable models quietly fail, and how to measure it well enough to fix it.

That work currently spans agent benchmarks, reward hacking in agent evaluation infrastructure, and how supportive language models shape a user's reliance on them. Before returning to research I spent six years building production ML at Nokia Bell Labs, Adani Group, and BlackRock, at 100M+ user scale.

Signal-Driven Observation for Long-Horizon Web Agents Shubham Gaur, Ian Lane
ICML 2026 Workshop on Failure Modes in Agentic AI (FAGEN), position paper
LLM Agents

Long-horizon web agents re-ingest tens of thousands of DOM tokens at every action step, eroding reasoning before the task completes. Decoupling observation from action — re-reading only when a URL change, a newly visible element, or an action failure fires — makes observation compression an architectural decision rather than a prompting one.
